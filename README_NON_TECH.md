Resume Builder and AI Placement Mentor

This project is a user-facing tool that helps job candidates prepare and improve their hiring materials and interview skills. It combines resume handling, automated assessments, and AI coaching to guide a candidate from resume upload to a personalized learning plan.

high level summary

- upload a resume and keep a profile with your chosen job role.
- take practice assessments and receive feedback based on your responses.
- get a guided learning roadmap that suggests topics and weekly practice tasks.
- use an AI mock interview to practice answering role-specific questions by voice or typing and receive a scored report with strengths, weak areas, and a short roadmap.

who this is for

This is for candidates who want a structured, supportive way to prepare for job applications and interviews. It helps people identify what to practice next and gives concrete, short-term goals.

what the system does in plain terms

1) account and profile
   - users sign up, log in, and set a preferred role. the app stores a simple profile so content and suggestions match your target job.

2) resume handling
   - you upload a resume file. the app extracts and saves the resume content so other features can reference skills and projects from your resume.

3) practice assessments
   - aptitude and coding assessments present questions for you to answer. after you finish, the system evaluates your answers and produces a report showing which areas you did well in and which need work.
   - the report includes a practical roadmap: a sequence of weekly study and practice tasks to improve specific weaknesses.

4) dsa agent
   - the coding agent provides programming problems, evaluates solutions, and suggests targeted practice topics and exercises based on your performance.

5) ai mock interview agent
   - the mock interview simulates a live interviewer by asking a set of 10 varied questions tailored to the chosen role and your resume context.
   - you answer by speaking or typing. the system captures your answers and, when you finish, generates a scored report with feedback and a short learning roadmap.
   - if you skip questions, the system treats them as unanswered and produces a lower readiness score with a practical plan to begin practicing.

6) how feedback and roadmaps are presented
   - after each assessment or interview, the system summarizes strengths and weaknesses in everyday language and gives a weekly plan with focused practice tasks.
   - the idea is to turn broad weaknesses into small, actionable steps you can follow for a few weeks.

how a typical user flows through the app

- sign up and set your target role.
- upload your resume so the system can pick relevant skills and projects.
- try an aptitude or coding assessment to see current strengths and weaknesses.
- run a mock interview to practice speaking and structuring answers.
- review the generated report and follow the suggested roadmap over several weeks.

key limitations you should know

- the AI feedback is coaching-oriented and not a final hiring decision. it suggests likely improvements rather than certifying readiness.
- voice interactions depend on the browser and device offering speech recognition and synthesis.
- the AI evaluation uses an external model when configured; otherwise, a deterministic fallback provides reasonable but simpler feedback.
